What is FairSeal 什麼是 FairSeal

Verifiable randomness infrastructure. Cryptographic proof that any randomized selection — slot spin, loot drop, gacha pull, AI agent decision — was committed before the outcome was knowable and was not tampered with.

可驗證隨機性基礎設施。密碼學證明：任何隨機選擇在結果可知前已承諾，且未被竄改。

Core primitive: Committed Selection Receipts (CSR) — compact proof locking selection rules to a future randomness source, verified client-side.

核心原語：承諾式選擇收據（CSR） — 將選擇規則鎖定至未來隨機源的簡潔證明。

Three conditions define our market / 三大適用條件：

  • Randomness is involved / 有隨機性
  • Stakes are attached (money, items, trust) / 有利害關係
  • Someone may dispute fairness / 有人可能質疑公平性

GLI-19 Compliance GLI-19 合規

Key finding: "Player shall not influence RNG" (§3.4.4) applies only to mechanical devices, not software RNG. Software requirements (§3.2–3.3) focus on distribution, independence, and cryptographic strength — all satisfied by HMAC-SHA256.

關鍵發現：「玩家不得影響 RNG」僅適用於機械裝置，不適用軟體 RNG。

  • Mode A (Provably Fair): Player seed in derivation — full verifiability
  • Mode B (GLI-Safe): Player seed as witness only — for regulated markets

模式 A：完全可驗證公平；模式 B：符合 MGA/UKGC 監管要求

Next: Pre-consultation with GLI or iTech Labs for Mode B certification.
